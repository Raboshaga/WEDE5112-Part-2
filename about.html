About Lakaze Kota Bar & Catering

Who We Are

Built around flavour and community

Lakaze Kota Bar & Catering is a Polokwane food business focused on gourmet Kotas, fast-food favourites and flexible catering for private and corporate events.

The website gives customers one place to learn about the business, explore services, view food examples and submit enquiries.

Mission

To prepare fresh, satisfying meals and reliable catering experiences that celebrate South African food culture.

Vision

To become a recognised Polokwane food brand known for quality, dependable service and memorable food experiences.

Values

Freshness, consistency, respect, customer care, local flavour and responsible food preparation.

Our Customers

Who We Serve

Students, working adults, families, event planners, local businesses and organisations looking for convenient food or catering.
